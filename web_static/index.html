📚 Signal & System Concepts Implemented

🔢 Discrete-Time Sampling

Voice is sampled at rate $f_s$ ($T_s = 1/f_s$). By the Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem, capturing all frequencies up to $f_s/2$ completely avoids spectral aliasing.

📐 LTI Butterworth Filters

4th-order IIR filters (High-pass 80 Hz, Low-pass 8 kHz) designed in the Z-domain: $H(z) = \prod \frac{b_k(z)}{a_k(z)}$. All poles are strictly inside $|z| < 1$, guaranteeing BIBO stability.

🎯 YIN Autocorrelation

Estimates fundamental period $T_0$ via cumulative mean normalized difference $d[\tau]$. Mathematically tied to the Power Spectral Density through the Wiener–Khinchin theorem.

🔄 Overlap-Add (OLA) Resampling

Pitch shifting resamples blocks by ratio $r = f_{\text{target}}/f_0$ using interpolation kernels. Windowing with a Hann window smooths block boundary discontinuities.

🔗 Cross-Correlation Phase Match

Consecutive frames are aligned via normalized cross-correlation $\rho[k]$ (matched filter theory) before cross-fading, eliminating phase jumps and boundary buzzing.

🎼 Logarithmic Pitch Grid

Musical frequency perception is logarithmic: $m = 12 \log_2(f/440) + 69$. Autotune performs non-linear quantization onto the discrete scale note lattice.
